# Team-health clustering - three buckets with a recommended next action

> KMeans is a clustering model that groups rows around a fixed number of centres.
> Here it sorts teams into three health buckets, and each bucket carries a fixed
> playbook of actions written by a human, not generated by the model.

## WHAT — Contract (normative)
Every line in this section is binding.

- `build_recommendations()` assigns every team one of three health buckets: `thriving`, `steady` or `at_risk`.
- Clustering reads five team metrics: average performance score, average KPI attainment, on-track share, average completion percentage and average comp ratio.
- Features are standardized before fitting, and KMeans runs with `n_clusters=3`, `n_init=10` and `random_state=42`, which keeps repeated runs identical.
- `_name_clusters()` ranks the three centroids by their mean standardized value: the highest becomes `thriving`, the lowest becomes `at_risk`, and the remaining one becomes `steady`.
- Each team inherits its bucket headline and three recommended actions from the fixed `CLUSTER_PLAYBOOK` table.
- Each record repeats the five metrics used, so a reader can see why the team landed in its bucket.
- Records are sorted by `team_id`, and `summary` reports the bucket distribution, model name, feature list and seed.
- `main()` writes `data/ai_recommendations.json` with `sort_keys=True`.

## WHAT — Verify intent (open questions for the human)
- Bucket names come from the mean centroid value across five standardized metrics, giving each metric equal weight. Should performance or KPI attainment weigh more than comp ratio?
- Naming is relative, so the weakest cluster is always labelled `at_risk` even when every team is healthy. Is that acceptable, or should the bucket be empty when no team is genuinely at risk?
- KMeans raises when fewer than three teams exist. Is a small organisation out of scope, or should the stage degrade to a single bucket?

## HOW — Acceptance (= tests)
- AC-1: Given the sample teams, when `build_recommendations()` runs twice, then every team receives the same bucket in both runs.
- AC-2: Given the output records, when each `cluster` value is checked, then it is one of `thriving`, `steady` or `at_risk`.
- AC-3: Given the output payload, when the distribution is summed, then it equals `n_teams`.
- AC-4: Given any record, when its `recommended_actions` are compared to `CLUSTER_PLAYBOOK`, then they match the entry for that record bucket.

## WHERE — Current implementation
- `scripts/cluster_team_health.py`

## Notes & limitations
- The playbook text is static per bucket, so two teams in the same bucket receive identical advice regardless of which metric put them there.
